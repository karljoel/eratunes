About EraTunez

Empowering African music, one stream at a time

Our Story

EraTunez was born from a simple idea: create a platform where African artists can share their music with the world and get paid fairly. Founded in 2024, we've grown into a vibrant community of musicians and music lovers across East Africa and beyond.

We believe that great music deserves a great platform. EraTunez is built for artists, by people who understand the challenges of the African music industry. Our mission is to bridge the gap between talented artists and their audience.

Our Mission

To empower African artists by providing a platform that offers fair compensation, global reach, and tools to grow their music career. We're committed to preserving and promoting the rich musical heritage of Africa while embracing modern sounds.

Our Vision

To become the leading music streaming platform in Africa, connecting millions of listeners with authentic African music while ensuring artists earn a sustainable living from their craft.
