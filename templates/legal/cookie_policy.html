Cookie Policy

Last updated: May 27, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how EraTunez uses cookies and similar technologies to recognize you when you visit our platform. It explains what these technologies are and why we use them, as well as your rights to control our use of them.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small data files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. Cookies are widely used by website owners to make their websites work more efficiently, as well as to provide reporting information.

Why Do We Use Cookies?

We use cookies for several reasons:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the platform to function properly (login, player, queue)
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings like dark/light mode
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how users interact with EraTunez
  • Personalization Cookies: Enable personalized recommendations and listening history

Types of Cookies We Use

Cookie Name Purpose Duration
sessionid Maintains your login session Session
csrftoken Protects against cross-site request forgery 1 year
eratunez_theme Remembers your dark/light mode preference 1 year
eratunez_player_state Saves your current song and queue position 1 year

Third-Party Cookies

We may also use cookies from third-party services:

  • YouTube: For embedded music videos
  • Bootstrap CDN: For platform styling
  • Font Awesome: For icons

Your Cookie Choices

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • See what cookies are stored
  • Delete all cookies
  • Block cookies from specific sites
  • Block all cookies

However, disabling essential cookies may affect platform functionality, such as staying logged in or saving your player preferences.
